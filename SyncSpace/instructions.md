This is a mostly standard calendar app

you can use the toggles on the sidebar to filter which events you would like to show

you can use the quick actions on the side or click on a day to open the creation menu
the only non-standard thing is anonymity at the bottom, this means that any group this event is shared to will see it as anonymous

You can also share events by pressing share event, here you can select from all your events and all of your groups, and the anonymous toggle here means the event is only anonymous for this group

if you navigate to the group tab, you can create groups.
you can use comma separated usernames, and then a group will appear at the bottom

when you click on a group card, you can edit which events are shared with that group, as well as already shared events.
