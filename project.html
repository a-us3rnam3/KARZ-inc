SyncSpace

Name: Henry Moore

Org Affil: McMaster Student

Users will be McMaster Students, likely to use the app on both computer and mobile. I believe they will often view data on mobile, but input data on a desktop. Easy importing of class schedules.

Static information will be a miniature tutorial.

Will have a login page for each user, after loggining in, you will be able to see a general page of every calendar collection you have (friend groups).

This is a calendar app; the interface should be simple and to the point without any extra and unnecessary buttons and random functionality. The app should have a clear and distinct visual hierarchy where upcoming events are labelled clearly and stand out in size/shape/colour. The colour palette should be muted, without loud colours and bold fonts. The pages must maintain a modern look, a clean and sharp appearance without sacrificing functionality. There should be purposeful animations, like when finishing a task, there should be a slight animation to reference the completion of a task that adds a nice, unique touch without being too over the top. The calendar should also be simple and not appear too busy, even if the user does have many tasks throughout the day. The app should also have a visual distinction between your own events and the events of your peers.

The client would want their own schedule to be presented to the user, as well as other users' schedules to be presented to them. They would want to see the current date and time, as well as future day plans.

  • A nice tool would be to be able to view an individual day, simply to see where everyone is and will be at a specific time.
  • A calculator to find spots when everyone is free
  • A system where you can list the importance of the event, and the free time optimizer can calculate a timeslot with minimal overlap of less important events. (could filter the importance of events to determine what can be removed)
  • when you're schedule is in the same location as another user, you will get a lil ping to notify that you're in the same area (this is assumeing you are following your schedule to a T)
