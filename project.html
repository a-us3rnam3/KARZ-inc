SyncSpace

Client: Henry Moore — McMaster Student

Users will be McMaster students, likely to use the app on both computer and mobile. They will often view data on mobile, but input data on a desktop. Easy importing of class schedules will be important.

Static information will include a miniature tutorial.

The app will have a login page for each user. After logging in, users will be able to see a general page showing every calendar collection they have, such as friend groups.

This is a calendar app, so the interface should be simple and to the point without extra unnecessary buttons or random functionality. The app should have a clear and distinct visual hierarchy where upcoming events are labelled clearly and stand out in size, shape, or colour. The colour palette should be muted, without loud colours or bold fonts. The pages must maintain a modern look and a clean, sharp appearance without sacrificing functionality.

There should be purposeful animations. For example, when finishing a task, there could be a slight animation to show completion in a way that adds a nice unique touch without being too over the top. The calendar should also stay simple and not look too busy, even if the user has many tasks throughout the day. The app should also have a visual distinction between your own events and the events of your peers.

The client would want their own schedule to be presented to the user, as well as other users' schedules. They would want to see the current date and time, as well as future day plans.

  • A useful tool would be the ability to view an individual day, simply to see where everyone is and will be at a specific time.
  • A calculator to find spots when everyone is free.
  • A system where you can list the importance of an event, and the free time optimizer can calculate a timeslot with minimal overlap of less important events.
  • When your schedule is in the same location as another user, you could get a small notification to show that you're in the same area, assuming both users are following their schedules exactly.
